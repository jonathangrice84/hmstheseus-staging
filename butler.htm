In Memory of
Stoker Petty Officer Mechanic FREDERICK THOMAS BUTLER

P/KX 88794, H.M.S. Theseus., Royal Navy
who died age 29
on 20 May 1947
Husband of Jane Butler, of Liverpool.


Remembered with honour
PORTSMOUTH NAVAL MEMORIAL


IN HONOUR OF THE NAVY AND TO THE ABIDING MEMORY OF THOSE RANKS AND RATINGS OF THIS PORT WHO LAID DOWN THEIR LIVES IN THE DEFENCE OF THE EMPIRE AND HAVE NO OTHER GRAVE THAN THE SEA

PO. Butler sadly died from heat stroke whilst sailing from Nancowry to Penang on the journey out to the Far East. He was buried at sea.
